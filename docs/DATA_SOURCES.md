## Policy on Unofficial Sources
LexAssist does NOT index:
- Legal advice forums
- Wikipedia articles
- Generic law student notes
- Commercial blog posts

All ingested text MUST be derived directly from official government gazettes, Ministry publications, or the India Code repository.
